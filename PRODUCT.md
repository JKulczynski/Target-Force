# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Two groups equally. (1) An advocacy/communications agency (Piotr's team) running several campaigns at once for clients, at a laptop in an office. (2) From November: the client itself (an NGO, association or company) setting up a campaign on its own, rarely and without practice. Job to be done: get a message in front of decision makers (MPs, MEPs, local government) in a credible, personal way, then prove the effect.

## Product Purpose
Target Force runs a decision-maker campaign end to end: describe the goal (templates, AI proposes who decides and when the Sejm sits), generate and approve messages, pick recipients (Sejm, European Parliament, local government, ministries, own list), personalise with a local argument, test, send in batches or on autopilot, remind, detect clicks and replies (IMAP), log impact (meetings, interpellations from the Sejm API), and report to the client. A public action page lets residents write to their own MP from their own mailbox (variant A, 07.10.2026). Success: a first-time user launches a credible campaign without getting lost, and the client gets a report that shows impact.

## Brand Personality
Campaign headquarters. Serious, decisive, professional: the tool of people who run campaigns and lobbying for a living. Concrete, numbers-first, a sense of influence. Three words: decisive, credible, focused.

## Anti-references
- A government website (gov.pl feel): bureaucratic, dull, institutional.
- A mass-mailing / spam tool: anything that makes the recipient or the client distrust the sender.
- (Implied by the brief) the current generic grey-white Tailwind card template.

## Design Principles
1. Credibility first: every screen should make sending feel responsible, never like blasting.
2. Guide, don't dump: the next step is always obvious, for the agency veteran and the first-time client alike.
3. Numbers carry the story: progress, recipients, clicks and replies are the hero, not decoration.
4. Calm under pressure: dense where needed, but quiet; colour only for state and action.
5. Safe by default: irreversible actions (real sends, automation) are clearly distinct from tests.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus states, full keyboard use, reduced-motion respected. Polish UI copy.
